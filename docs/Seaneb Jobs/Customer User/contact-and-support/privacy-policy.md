# Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Policy detailing how user data is collected, stored, processed, and safeguarded.

### Data We Collect
- **Identity Details:** Name, mobile number, email address, city, and state.
- **Career Information:** Work history, education, skills, and resume documents.
- **Platform Activity:** Job search queries, saved jobs, and submitted applications.

### How We Protect Your Data
- **No Data Resale:** We never sell personal contact information to third-party telemarketers or advertisers.
- **Encrypted Transmission:** All platform traffic is secured via modern TLS encryption.
- **Data Erasure:** You can permanently delete your account and personal data at any time via Settings > Delete Account.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Manage your data visibility preferences under Settings > Privacy.
2. Contact privacy@seaneb.com for any data access inquiries.

### Expected Results
Total transparency regarding your digital privacy and data protection rights.

### Common Problems & Solutions

| **Request personal data copy** | Email privacy@seaneb.com with your registered mobile number. |

### Helpful Tips
