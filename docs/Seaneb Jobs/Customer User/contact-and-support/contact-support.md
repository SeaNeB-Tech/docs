# Contact Support

How to contact our customer care team for technical help, verification, or questions.

### Getting Help
Our customer support team is available Monday through Saturday from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST.

#### Contact Channels:
- **Email:** support@seaneb.com
- **Employer Support:** business-support@seaneb.com
- **Website:** https://jobs.seaneb.com/

When contacting support, please include your registered 10-digit mobile number and a clear description of your issue.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Compose an email to support@seaneb.com.
2. State your registered mobile number in the message.
3. Describe the issue and attach screenshots if applicable.
4. Send the email and receive a response within 2-4 business hours.

### Expected Results
Helpful assistance from Seaneb customer support experts.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No response after 4 hours** | Check your email spam folder or send a follow-up email quoting your ticket number. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Providing clear screenshots helps our technical team solve your issue much faster.
