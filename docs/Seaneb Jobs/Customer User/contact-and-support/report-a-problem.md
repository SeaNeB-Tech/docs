# Report a Problem

Submit technical bug reports, display glitches, or app issues to our engineering team.

### What to Include in a Bug Report
- **Device & Browser:** e.g., Samsung Galaxy A14 / Chrome for Android.
- **Page URL:** Where the error happened (e.g., https://jobs.seaneb.com/profile).
- **Description:** What you clicked and what happened unexpectedly.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Take a screenshot of the error message or visual glitch.
2. Email details to bugs@seaneb.com.
3. Our engineering team reviews reports during regular update cycles.

### Expected Results
Direct contribution to making Seaneb Jobs better and more reliable.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Page appears frozen** | Try hard-refreshing your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R) or clear browser cache. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always check if your browser has pending updates, as newer versions fix many common display issues.
