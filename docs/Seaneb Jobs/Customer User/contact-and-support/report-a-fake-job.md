# Report a Fake Job

Urgent reporting channel for fee-demanding scammers, fake employers, and fraudulent listings.

### High-Priority Fraud Reporting
> **Reminder:** Seaneb Jobs is completely free. We never charge job seekers any fees. Any recruiter asking for money is committing fraud.

#### What We Need to Take Immediate Action:
- Job Title and Company Name.
- Phone number or WhatsApp handle used by the caller.
- Screenshots of payment requests, QR codes, or bank account numbers.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open the fraudulent job posting.
2. Click the red "Report Fake Job" link.
3. Upload screenshots of payment demands or chat logs.
4. Submit the report. Our Trust & Safety team reviews high-priority fraud reports within 60 minutes.

### Expected Results
Immediate suspension of fraudulent accounts to protect other job seekers.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Paid money to a scammer** | File an immediate complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and notify your bank to stop the transaction. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Never pay any money for job registration, interview scheduling, or uniform charges.
