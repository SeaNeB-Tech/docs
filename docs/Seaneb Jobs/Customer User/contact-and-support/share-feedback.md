# Share Feedback

Share suggestions, feature ideas, and feedback to help us improve Seaneb Jobs.

### We Value Your Feedback
Seaneb Jobs is built for everyday people and local businesses. We actively review feedback to build new features:
- Suggestions for new Resume Builder designs.
- Requests for additional regional languages.
- Improvements for local neighborhood search.
- Usability enhancements for mobile devices.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Write down your suggestion and how it would improve your job search experience.
2. Email feedback@seaneb.com with subject "User Feedback".
3. Our product management team reviews all suggestions during weekly design sprints.

### Expected Results
Your ideas directly influence upcoming updates to the platform.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Need urgent technical support** | Please use support@seaneb.com instead of feedback for technical troubleshooting. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Be as specific as possible about how your suggestion would help other job seekers.
