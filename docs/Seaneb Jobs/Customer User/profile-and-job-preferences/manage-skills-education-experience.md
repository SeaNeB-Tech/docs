# Manage Skills, Education & Experience

Detailed instructions on adding, editing, and organizing your educational history, work experience, and competencies.

![Mobile View - Manage Education & Skills Details](/img/mobile/education.png)


### Structuring Your Career History
- **Work Experience:** List company name, your designation, start and end dates, and 2-3 key duties.
- **Education:** Select your highest degree, school or university name, passing year, and percentage/grade.
- **Skills:** Add both technical skills (e.g., Tally, Typing, Driving) and soft skills (e.g., Communication, Customer Service).

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. In "My Profile", scroll to the "Experience" section and click "Add Experience".
2. Enter details for each past job and save.
3. Scroll to "Education" and click "Add Education".
4. Scroll to "Skills" and select relevant skill tags from the list or type custom skills.
5. Click "Save Profile".

### Expected Results
A complete, structured professional background that auto-populates your resume.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cannot find my specific degree in dropdown** | Select the closest equivalent (e.g., Graduate) and type your specific degree in the field provided. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Add at least 5 skills to improve match accuracy with employer job requirements.
