# Manage Job Preferences

Configure your target job roles, minimum salary expectations, and preferred commute distance.

### Configurable Preference Parameters
- **Target Job Roles:** Select up to 5 designations matching your skills (e.g., Accountant, Office Assistant, Sales Executive).
- **Minimum Expected Salary:** Set your bottom-line monthly take-home salary.
- **Work Shift:** Day Shift, Night Shift, Rotational Shift, Flexible.
- **Commute Distance:** Choose your preferred travel radius (within 5 km, 10 km, or open to entire city).

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open "My Profile" > "Job Preferences".
2. Select your preferred job roles and industries.
3. Input your expected salary.
4. Specify preferred work areas in your city.
5. Click "Save Preferences".

### Expected Results
Your personalized job recommendation feed on the homepage updates to match your criteria.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Too few recommended jobs** | Increase your commute radius or add broader job role categories. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Be realistic with salary expectations based on local averages in your city.
