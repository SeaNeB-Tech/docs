# Profile Completion

Learn how profile completion scores work and how to achieve a 100% score for maximum visibility.


![Profile Completion Progress & Checklist](/img/web/profile.png)


### Completion Points Breakdown
- **Personal Details & Photo:** 20%
- **Work Experience:** 25%
- **Education & Certifications:** 20%
- **Skills:** 20%
- **Job Preferences (Salary & Location):** 15%

Profiles with 100% completion appear at the top of employer talent search results and get up to 4x more interview invitations.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Mobile App Experience

![Mobile View - Profile Details & Job Seeker Status](/img/mobile/my-profile.png)

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Visit "My Profile".
2. Check your completion percentage bar.
3. Click on sections marked with a warning badge.
4. Fill in the requested details and save.
5. Reach 100% to earn the "Complete Profile" badge.

### Expected Results
Maximum visibility across the Seaneb employer talent directory.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Score stuck at 85%** | Ensure you added at least 3 skill tags and selected your expected monthly salary. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** A complete profile means employers have all the information they need without having to ask basic questions during screening calls.
