# Seeking Jobs — How It Works

Understand how the "Seeking Job" toggle switch broadcasts your availability to active recruiters.


![Seeking Job Toggle Switch on Profile](/img/web/profile.png)


### How the Toggle Functions
- **ON (Green):** Signals to employers that you are actively interviewing and ready to join. Your card displays an "Actively Seeking" badge.
- **OFF (Gray):** Hides you from active employer talent discovery while preserving all your saved jobs, applications, and resumes.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Locate the "Seeking Job" toggle switch in your "My Profile" header.
2. Click to toggle it ON when looking for employment.
3. Select your notice period (Immediate, 15 Days, 30 Days).
4. Toggle it OFF once you accept a job offer.

### Expected Results
Your availability status updates in real time across the employer database.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Still receiving inquiries shortly after turning OFF** | Employers who already downloaded your resume prior to toggling off may still call you from previous searches. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Keep your status as "Immediate" joining if you are unemployed to get priority interview calls.
