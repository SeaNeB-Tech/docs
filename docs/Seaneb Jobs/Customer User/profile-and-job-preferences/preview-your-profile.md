# Preview Your Profile

See exactly how employers view your profile when browsing candidate search results.


![Preview Profile Button and Header View](/img/web/profile.png)


### Why Preview?
Previewing your profile allows you to inspect your information from a recruiter's perspective, checking for missing details, formatting errors, or outdated work history before applying.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Navigate to "My Profile".
2. Click the "Preview Profile" button located near the top of the screen.
3. Review the employer view of your profile.
4. Check that your skills, experience, and education appear correctly.
5. Click "Edit Profile" if you notice anything that needs adjustment.

### Expected Results
Confidence that prospective employers see a professional, error-free presentation.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Photo appears stretched** | Upload a square photo (1:1 aspect ratio) for best results. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always preview your profile after making updates to ensure everything looks neat and professional.
