# Update Personal Information

How to update your legal name, date of birth, gender, address, and profile photo.


![Personal Information Fields and Edit View](/img/web/create-account.png)


### What Personal Information is Displayed
Employers review your basic details to confirm eligibility and commute practicality. Your contact information is kept confidential until you submit an application or accept an interview invite.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Go to "My Profile" > "Personal Information".
2. Update your full name, city, state, and pin code.
3. Upload or replace your profile photo with a clear, professional headshot.
4. Click "Save Changes".

### Expected Results
Accurate personal records displayed on your profile and resume.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cannot change registered mobile number here** | Mobile numbers must be updated via Settings > Change Mobile Number with OTP verification. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Use a high-quality photo with a neutral background to make a positive first impression.
