# Can I Edit My Profile After Registration?

Learn how to edit your details anytime as your career grows and contact information changes.

### Yes. You can edit any part of your profile at any time.
You can update your work history, add new certifications, change expected salary, or revise contact preferences whenever you need.

Updates take effect immediately across all future applications and refresh your downloadable resume automatically.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Go to "My Profile".
2. Click the "Edit" button next to any section you wish to update.
3. Make your changes.
4. Click "Save Profile".

### Expected Results
Updated information displayed immediately across your account.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Previous applications still show old resume** | Applications already submitted contain a snapshot of your profile at the time of submission. New applications will use the updated version. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Keep your expected salary and preferred location updated as your career goals evolve.
