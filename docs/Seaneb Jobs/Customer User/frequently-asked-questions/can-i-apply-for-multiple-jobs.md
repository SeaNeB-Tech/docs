# Can I Apply for Multiple Jobs?

Learn about application limits, multi-job submissions, and strategic job hunting.

### Yes. You can apply to as many different vacancies as you want.
There is no limit on the number of unique employers or distinct job postings you can apply to. However, we advise focusing on positions that align with your genuine skills and commute capabilities to maximize interview success.

> **Note:** You cannot submit duplicate applications to the *same* job posting; the system maintains your single active submission with that employer.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Search for jobs across your city.
2. Review requirements for each role.
3. Click "Apply Now" on each position of interest.

### Expected Results
Multiple active applications tracked simultaneously in your Applications dashboard.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Too many applications without responses** | Improve your profile completion score and add specific skills relevant to your target roles. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Applying to 5-10 well-matched local jobs gives you the best chance of receiving multiple interview invitations.
