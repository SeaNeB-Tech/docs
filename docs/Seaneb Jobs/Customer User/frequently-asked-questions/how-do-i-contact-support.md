# How Do I Contact Support?

All available ways to reach our customer support team for help.

### Official Support Channels
- **Email:** support@seaneb.com (Responses within 2 to 4 business hours)
- **Support Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST
- **Online Ticket Form:** Visit our [Contact Support](../contact-and-support/contact-support) page.
- **Headquarters:** Seaneb Technologies Pvt Ltd, India.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Choose your preferred contact channel.
2. Provide your registered 10-digit mobile number and any relevant Job ID.
3. Our team will investigate and provide a prompt resolution.

### Expected Results
Direct assistance from trained platform specialists.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Urgent account verification** | Include "URGENT" in your email subject line for priority handling. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always correspond using your registered email address for faster verification.
