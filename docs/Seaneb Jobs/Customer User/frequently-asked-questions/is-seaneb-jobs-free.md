# Is Seaneb Jobs Free?

Understand our 100% free policy for job seekers and transparent pricing for businesses.

### Yes. Seaneb Jobs is 100% free for job seekers.

#### Completely Free Features for Candidates:
- Searching and browsing unlimited local jobs.
- Applying directly to verified employers.
- Creating, previewing, and downloading PDF resumes with no watermarks.
- Receiving interview alerts via SMS, Email, and WhatsApp.
- Tracking application statuses in real time.

We will **never** ask job seekers for application fees, subscription charges, or resume download payments.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Sign up for free using your 10-digit mobile number.
2. Use all job seeking and resume builder tools with zero charges.

### Expected Results
Full access to employment features without financial obligations.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Someone asked for money claiming to represent Seaneb** | Report them immediately to support@seaneb.com; Seaneb is strictly free for job seekers. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always report any employer who asks for money under the guise of registration or interview fees.
