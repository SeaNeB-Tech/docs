# How Can I Improve My Profile Completion?

Practical steps to boost your profile score to 100% and rank at the top of candidate searches.

### Top 5 Ways to Reach 100% Completion
1. **Upload a Clear Headshot Photo:** Profiles with photos receive 3x more recruiter views.
2. **Detail Work History:** Add at least one past or current job with 2-3 sentences explaining your daily duties.
3. **Add at Least 5 Skills:** Include tools you know (e.g., Tally, MS Excel, Driving, Retail POS).
4. **Specify Preferred Locations:** Select neighborhoods where you can commute comfortably.
5. **Set Expected Monthly Salary:** Specify your realistic target compensation.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open "My Profile".
2. Look for sections marked with a yellow warning badge.
3. Fill in education, experience, and skills.
4. Save your profile to unlock the 100% complete badge.

### Expected Results
Higher visibility and top ranking in employer talent searches.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Score stuck at 85%** | Ensure you completed both "Highest Education" and "Job Preferences". |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Complete profiles receive priority placement in employer candidate recommendations.
