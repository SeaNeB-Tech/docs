# What Should I Do If a Job Looks Suspicious?

Safety rules and reporting procedures if you encounter suspicious recruiters.

### Critical Safety Rules
1. **Never pay money for a job.** Legitimate employers never charge fees for interviews, registration, or uniforms.
2. **Do not share bank OTPs or UPI PINs.**
3. **Do not click suspicious links** in unverified WhatsApp messages.
4. **Click "Report a Job"** on the listing page immediately.
5. **Block the caller** on your phone.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Stop all communication with the suspicious party.
2. File an in-app report using the "Report Fake Job" button.
3. Notify support@seaneb.com with chat screenshots and phone numbers.

### Expected Results
Immediate investigation and suspension of fraudulent accounts.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lost money to a fraudster** | Register an immediate complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and your local police station. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always communicate through official Seaneb platform features to keep your personal contact details safe.
