# How Do I Delete My Account?

A quick summary of permanent account deletion and data removal.

To delete your account permanently:
1. Open **Settings** > **Account & Security**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **Delete Account**.
3. Verify your identity using an SMS OTP.
4. Confirm deletion.

All your personal details, resumes, applications, and saved jobs will be permanently deleted from active databases.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Go to user menu > Settings.
2. Click Delete Account in the Account tab.
3. Enter the verification code received on your phone.

### Expected Results
Complete erasure of your account and data.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Only want to pause calls** | Toggle "Seeking Job" to OFF instead of deleting your entire account. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Download your free PDF resume before deleting your account so you have an offline copy.
