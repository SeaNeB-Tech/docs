# How Long Does a Job Remain Active?

Understand the standard 30-day listing lifespan and how it protects candidates from stale postings.

Every job posted on Seaneb Jobs remains publicly active for **30 calendar days** from the date of publishing.

### Why 30 Days?
Enforcing a 30-day lifecycle prevents outdated vacancies from cluttering search feeds and ensures job seekers only spend time applying to companies that are actively hiring right now.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Check the "Expires in X days" counter on any job card.
2. Apply promptly to jobs with fewer than 5 days remaining.

### Expected Results
Fresh, responsive job listings across the platform.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Job closed early** | Employers often mark jobs as closed once they receive sufficient qualified applicants. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Check the job search feed daily to discover new openings as soon as they are published.
