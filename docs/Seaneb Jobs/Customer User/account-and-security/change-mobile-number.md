# Change Mobile Number

How to safely migrate your account, applications, and saved resumes to a new phone number.

### Security Requirements
Because your phone number is your login identity, migrating to a new number requires OTP verification on **both** your current registered number and your new mobile number to prevent unauthorized takeovers.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open "Settings" > "Account & Security".
2. Click "Change Mobile Number" next to your registered number.
3. Enter the verification OTP sent to your current registered number.
4. Type your new 10-digit mobile number and click "Send OTP".
5. Enter the verification code received on your new number to confirm ownership.

### Expected Results
Your account is linked to the new number. All application histories, resumes, and bookmarks are preserved.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lost access to old number** | Contact Seaneb Support with your identity proof for manual account verification and transfer. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Make sure your new number is active and capable of receiving SMS before starting the change process.
