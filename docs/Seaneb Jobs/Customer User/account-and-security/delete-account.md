# Delete Account

Permanently remove your account, resumes, and personal information from Seaneb Jobs.


![Delete Account Tab in Settings](/img/web/Settings.png)


### Permanent Deletion Warning
> **Warning:** Account deletion is **permanent and cannot be undone**. Deleting your account will permanently erase your profile, saved resumes, application histories, and bookmarks.

#### If You Only Want to Pause Job Seeking:
Consider turning off the **"Seeking Job"** toggle switch on your profile instead. This hides your profile from employers without losing your saved resume data.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Go to "Settings" > "Account & Security".
2. Scroll down to the Danger Zone at the bottom.
3. Click "Delete Account".
4. Select a reason for leaving.
5. Enter the confirmation OTP sent to your registered mobile number.
6. Click "Permanently Delete Account".

### Expected Results
Your personal data is immediately removed from active platform databases in compliance with privacy regulations.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Deleted by mistake** | Deleted accounts cannot be restored; you will need to register a new account. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Download your free PDF resume before deleting your account so you keep a copy of your career document.
