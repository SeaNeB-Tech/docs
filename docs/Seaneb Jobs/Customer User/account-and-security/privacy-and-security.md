# Privacy & Security

How Seaneb Jobs protects your contact details, personal documents, and data privacy.


![Account Privacy & Security Settings](/img/web/Settings.png)


### Our Core Privacy Standards
- **No Public Mobile Numbers:** Your phone number is never shown publicly on search engines. It is only shared with verified employers whose jobs you apply to or when you make your profile public to verified employers.
- **End-to-End Encryption:** All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is secured using modern TLS 1.3 encryption.
- **No Data Reselling:** Seaneb never sells user information to third-party telemarketers, loan aggregators, or insurance agents.
- **Verified Businesses Only:** Employers must verify their identity before gaining access to talent search databases.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Review your visibility settings under Settings > Privacy.
2. Audit active devices logged into your account.
3. Click "Log Out of All Devices" if you previously used a public computer or cyber café.

### Expected Results
Full control and confidence regarding your personal data protection.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Suspected unauthorized access** | Change your mobile number or contact support immediately to terminate all active sessions. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always log out when accessing your account from a public computer or shared cyber café PC.
