# Forgot Password / Lost Access

How to recover your account if you lost your phone, SIM card, or registered device.

### Passwordless Architecture
Because Seaneb Jobs does not use static passwords, there is no password to forget! If you lost your SIM card or mobile phone, follow these recovery procedures.

#### Recovery Options:
1. **SIM Replacement (Fastest):** Visit your telecom carrier store (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL) to get a duplicate SIM with your existing number. Once active, log in normally via OTP.
2. **Support Account Recovery:** Contact Seaneb Support with your legal name and government photo ID (Aadhaar or Voter ID) to migrate your account to a new number.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. On the login screen, click "Need help logging in?".
2. Select "Lost access to registered phone number".
3. Provide your registered details and alternate contact number.
4. Our support team will verify your identity within 24 hours.

### Expected Results
Restoration of your Seaneb Jobs account and profile history.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Support verification delay** | Ensure uploaded photo ID matches the full legal name on your Seaneb profile. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Getting a replacement SIM card from your telecom operator is usually completed in less than 2 hours.
