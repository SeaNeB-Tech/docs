# Blocked or Suspended Account

Understand why accounts may be suspended and how to submit an appeal to restore access.

### Reasons for Account Suspension
To ensure a trustworthy community, accounts that violate our terms may be suspended:
- Submitting repetitive fake applications or spam messages.
- Impersonating other candidates or submitting forged credentials.
- Inappropriate communication with employers or support personnel.
- Using automated bots or scrapers.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Review the reason stated on the suspension banner upon login.
2. Prepare your identity documents and explanation.
3. Email support@seaneb.com with subject "Account Appeal - [Mobile Number]".
4. Our Trust & Safety team will review your case within 48 business hours.

### Expected Results
Fair evaluation and possible reinstatement for compliant users.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Appeal rejected** | Decisions involving confirmed fraudulent activities are final and cannot be overturned. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always provide truthful qualifications and accurate contact information to maintain an account in good standing.
