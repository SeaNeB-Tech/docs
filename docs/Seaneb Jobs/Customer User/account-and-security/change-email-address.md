# Change Email Address

Update or verify your primary email address for application receipts and digests.

### Why Keep Email Updated?
An active email address ensures you receive comprehensive interview appointment letters, tax receipts (for employers), and account security alerts.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Navigate to "Settings" > "Account Details".
2. Enter your new email address in the Email field.
3. Click "Verify Email".
4. Check your inbox for a confirmation email and click the verification link.

### Expected Results
Your email address is updated across your profile and notifications.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Verification email not arriving** | Check your Spam or Junk folder and ensure you typed the address correctly. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Use a professional email address containing your name (e.g., `rahul.sharma@gmail.com`) for a positive impression on recruiters.
