# Update Your Profile

Your Seaneb Jobs profile acts as your live digital resume. Keeping it updated ensures local employers have accurate information about your qualifications and contact details.


![My Profile Dashboard & Details](/img/web/profile.png)


### Key Profile Sections
1. **Personal Information:** Full Name, Current Location, Pin Code, and Primary Mobile Number.
2. **Work Experience:** Current and past job designations, employer company names, daily responsibilities, and total years of experience.
3. **Education:** Highest qualification achieved (10th, 12th, Graduate, Post Graduate, Diploma, ITI), institution name, and completion year.
4. **Skills:** Key skills like Tally, MS Excel, Driving, Retail Sales, Cooking, Telecalling, etc.
5. **Job Preferences:** Expected salary, preferred job roles, and preferred commute distance.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Log in to your Seaneb Jobs account.
2. Click on your profile avatar or name in the top right corner and select "My Profile".
3. Click the "Edit" pencil icon next to the section you wish to update.
4. Enter your revised employment history, newly acquired skills, or updated address.
5. Click "Save Changes" at the bottom of the section.

### Expected Results
Your profile updates immediately across all active job applications and refreshes your downloadable PDF resume.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Changes not saving** | Ensure all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) are filled out. |
| **Profile photo not uploading** | Ensure your photo is in JPG or PNG format and smaller than 5 MB. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Keep your expected salary realistic for your local city market to get the highest volume of interview invitations.
