# What is Seaneb Jobs?

Seaneb Jobs is an innovative hyperlocal employment platform created to connect job seekers directly with verified local businesses, retail stores, offices, and MSMEs across India without employment agency middlemen or recruiter fees.


![Seaneb Jobs Portal Homepage](/img/web/home.png)


### Understanding the Platform
Finding a nearby job in your city or neighborhood can often be challenging with traditional job boards that prioritize large multinational corporate jobs. Seaneb Jobs solves this by focusing on local employment opportunities where you live.

#### Key Benefits for Job Seekers:
- **Direct Employer Contact:** Connect directly with hiring managers and shop owners without paying any recruitment agency commissions.
- **100% Free Resume Builder:** Build, edit, and download clean, professional PDF resumes with zero hidden charges or watermarks.
- **Hyperlocal Matching:** Discover open roles within comfortable walking or commuting distance in your neighborhood or district.
- **Real-Time Status Tracking:** Stay informed with live updates on whether your application has been viewed, shortlisted, or scheduled for an interview.
- **Mobile-First Experience:** Accessible on any smartphone via fast, passwordless mobile OTP login.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Visit the official website at https://jobs.seaneb.com/ on your phone or computer.
2. Browse available job vacancies by city, category, or job role.
3. Register for free using your 10-digit mobile number and receive a quick verification code via SMS or WhatsApp.
4. Complete your profile and download your free professional resume.

### Expected Results
You gain immediate access to local job openings and can start applying directly with verified employers.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Website not opening** | Check your internet connection and ensure JavaScript is enabled in your browser. |
| **Cannot find jobs in small towns** | Try searching by your broader district name or expanding your location filter. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Complete your profile to at least 80% to appear in employer talent search results and get direct interview calls.
