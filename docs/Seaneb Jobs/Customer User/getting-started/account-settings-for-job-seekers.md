# Account Settings for Job Seekers

The Account Settings panel gives you full control over your privacy, notification channels, visibility in employer searches, and account data.


![Job Seeker Account Settings & Preferences](/img/web/Settings.png)


### Available Settings Options
- **Seeking Job Toggle:** Switch your job-seeking status ON or OFF. When turned ON, verified employers can discover your profile. When turned OFF, your profile is hidden from talent searches while preserving your account.
- **Notification Preferences:** Select whether you want to receive job match alerts via SMS, WhatsApp, or Email.
- **Account Credentials:** Update your registered email address or initiate a mobile number transfer.
- **Delete Account:** Permanently erase your account and all associated resumes from the platform.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Navigate to your user menu in the top right corner.
2. Select "Settings" from the dropdown options.
3. Browse through the tabs: Preferences, Notifications, and Account Security.
4. Make your desired adjustments.
5. Click "Save Settings" to confirm your preferences.

### Expected Results
Your updated privacy and communication rules take effect immediately.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Still receiving notifications after disabling** | Verify that you clicked "Save Settings" after changing toggle switches. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** If you get hired, simply turn off the "Seeking Job" toggle instead of deleting your account. This allows you to reactivate your profile anytime in the future.
