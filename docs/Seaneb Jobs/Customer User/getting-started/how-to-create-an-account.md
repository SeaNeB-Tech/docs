# How to Create an Account

Creating your Seaneb Jobs account takes less than a minute. The platform uses secure, passwordless authentication so you never have to remember complex passwords.


![Seaneb Jobs Mobile Login Screen](/img/web/login.png)


![Complete Profile & Account Registration](/img/web/create-account.png)


### Registration Requirements
To create your job seeker account, you only need:
- A valid 10-digit Indian mobile number capable of receiving SMS messages.
- An internet-connected device (smartphone, tablet, or PC).
- Basic personal details (your full name and current city).

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Mobile App Experience

![Mobile View - Registration & Account Creation Screen](/img/mobile/create-acccount.png)

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open https://jobs.seaneb.com/ in your browser.
2. Click the "Login / Sign Up" button located in the top navigation bar.
3. Enter your 10-digit mobile phone number into the input field and click "Send OTP".
4. Check your mobile messages for a 6-digit verification code.
5. Enter the 6-digit OTP into the verification screen.
6. Provide your full legal name, select your current city, and click "Complete Registration".

### Expected Results
Your account is immediately created, and you are redirected to your personal dashboard where you can begin applying for jobs.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Did not receive OTP** | Wait for the 60-second timer to finish and tap "Resend OTP", or ensure your phone has good cellular signal. |
| **Entered wrong mobile number** | Click "Edit number" on the OTP screen to re-type your correct 10 digits. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always use your primary phone number that you carry daily, as employers will contact you on this number for interview calls.
