# Job Expiry

Learn about the 30-day listing lifespan on Seaneb Jobs and why vacancies expire.

### 30-Day Freshness Standard
To protect job seekers from applying to outdated vacancies, every listing on Seaneb Jobs remains active for **30 calendar days**.

After 30 days:
- The listing is delisted from search feeds.
- Employers can renew the posting if they are still hiring.
- Existing applicants can still be reviewed and contacted by the employer.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Check the "Expires in X days" counter on job cards.
2. Apply promptly to jobs approaching their expiry date to ensure your resume is reviewed before shortlisting closes.

### Expected Results
Protection against stale listings and ghost jobs.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Saved job expired before applying** | Check the employer profile to see if they published a renewed listing for the same role. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Sort search results by "Recently Posted" to find newly listed vacancies.
