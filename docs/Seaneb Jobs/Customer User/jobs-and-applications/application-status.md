# Application Status

A complete reference guide explaining what each application status badge means and what action you should take.


![Application Status Cards & Details](/img/web/applications.png)


### Status Reference Table

| Status Label | Badge Color | Meaning | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Applied** | Blue | Application delivered to employer. | Be patient. Keep applying to other suitable openings. |
| **Under Review** | Purple | Recruiter opened your profile. | Keep your phone available for incoming screening calls. |
| **Shortlisted** | Amber | You passed initial screening. | Review company profile and prepare for interview questions. |
| **Interview Scheduled** | Green | Interview confirmed with date and venue. | Attend interview on time with printed resume copies. |
| **Not Selected** | Gray | Employer chose another applicant. | Don't be discouraged; apply for other local vacancies. |
| **Withdrawn** | Muted | You cancelled your application. | No further action required. |

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Check the colored badge on your application card.
2. Read any notes attached by the employer.
3. Take the recommended action outlined in the table above.

### Expected Results
Clear understanding of your hiring stage with each employer.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Status shows Interview Scheduled but no venue given** | Contact the employer through the phone number listed on the card. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Never stop applying to other jobs while waiting for an interview response; multiple applications increase your chances of getting hired quickly.
