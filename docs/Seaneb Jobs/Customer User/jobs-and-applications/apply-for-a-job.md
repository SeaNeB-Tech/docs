# Apply for a Job

Step-by-step guidance on submitting your resume directly to verified employers. On mobile, use the **Slide to Apply** slider to apply instantly.


![Job Details Page - Apply Now on Desktop](/img/web/job-details.png)


### Application Options
Seaneb Jobs supports two convenient ways to apply:
1. **Direct Profile Application:** Your online profile and generated resume are sent directly into the employer's dashboard.
2. **Call / WhatsApp HR (when enabled):** Certain urgent local employers allow direct phone calls. Look for the "Call HR" or "WhatsApp HR" button on eligible listings.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Mobile App Experience

On mobile devices, Seaneb Jobs features an intuitive **Slide to Apply** swipe slider at the bottom of the job details page to ensure smooth and intentional applications without accidental taps.

![Mobile View - Slide to Apply on Job Details Screen](/img/mobile/job-details.png)

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open the job details page for the vacancy you want.
2. Click the blue "Apply Now" button on desktop, or swipe the **"Slide to Apply"** slider from left to right on mobile.
3. Select which resume to attach: your auto-generated Seaneb Resume or profile summary.
4. Answer any screening questions configured by the employer (e.g., "Do you own a two-wheeler?").
5. Confirm and submit your application.

### Expected Results
Your application is registered in your "Applied Jobs" tab and an SMS confirmation is sent to your phone.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Apply button is disabled** | Ensure your profile is completed to at least 60% and that you have entered your primary contact information. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always tailor your profile headline to match the job role you are applying for.
