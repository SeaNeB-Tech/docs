# Track Your Applications

Learn how to use the Applications dashboard to follow hiring progress across all your submissions.


![Tracking Application Status (Shortlisted, Applied)](/img/web/applications.png)


### Application Tracking Features
- **Status Filter Tabs:** Switch between "All", "Shortlisted", "Interviews", and "Archived".
- **Timeline View:** View exact dates and times when your application changed status.
- **Interview Details:** Access office venue address, interview timing, and HR contact numbers.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Mobile App Experience

![Mobile View - Application Tracking & Status Feed](/img/mobile/my-applications.png)

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Log in and click "Applications" in the navigation bar.
2. Filter by status tab to see where you have progressed.
3. Click on any application card to see recruiter feedback or interview schedules.
4. If an interview is scheduled, note down the venue address and contact person.

### Expected Results
Complete real-time visibility into all your job applications in one central dashboard.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Missed an interview call** | Open the application card and use the "Call HR" button to call back politely and explain. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Turn on WhatsApp notifications to receive interview reminders with direct Google Maps location links.
