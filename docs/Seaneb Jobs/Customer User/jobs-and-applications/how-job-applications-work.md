# How Job Applications Work

Understand the complete recruitment lifecycle on Seaneb Jobs from the moment you click Apply (or use Slide to Apply on mobile) until you get hired.


![Job Application Page & Apply Now Button](/img/web/job-details.png)


### The 5 Stages of an Application
```
[ Applied ] ──▶ [ Under Review ] ──▶ [ Shortlisted ] ──▶ [ Interview Scheduled ] ──▶ [ Hired ]
```

1. **Applied:** Your profile and resume are delivered to the employer's applicant inbox.
2. **Under Review:** The employer has opened and examined your application details.
3. **Shortlisted:** The recruiter decided your background fits the criteria and marked you for interview consideration.
4. **Interview Scheduled:** The employer sends an interview invite with date, time, and office address.
5. **Hired:** The employer selected you for the vacancy and marked recruitment complete.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Submit your application using the "Apply Now" button on desktop web or swipe the **"Slide to Apply"** slider on mobile.
2. Keep your phone accessible for recruiter phone screening calls.
3. Check your Applications tab for status transitions.
4. Attend your scheduled interview on time with printed copies of your resume.

### Expected Results
Transparent visibility into how hiring decisions are communicated.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Application stuck on Applied for over 2 weeks** | The employer may have already filled the opening or paused recruitment. Continue applying to other active roles. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Candidates who apply within 48 hours of a job being posted have the highest chance of getting shortlisted.
