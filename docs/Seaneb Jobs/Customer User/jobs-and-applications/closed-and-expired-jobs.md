# Closed & Expired Jobs

Understand what happens to your submitted applications when a job vacancy closes.

### Impact on Existing Candidates
- **If you already submitted an application:** Your resume remains safely preserved in the employer dashboard. The employer can still shortlist and call you for interviews.
- **If you bookmarked the job but did not apply:** The "Apply" button is deactivated and marked as "Job Closed".

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open "Applications" to view past submissions to closed jobs.
2. If a job is closed, click "Find Similar Jobs" to see other active vacancies in that category.

### Expected Results
Clear expectations about application status after job listings conclude.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Recruiter called for a closed role** | Employers often retain qualified candidate resumes for future openings. This is standard professional practice. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Keep your resume updated even after applying, as employers often search past applicant pools for new roles.
