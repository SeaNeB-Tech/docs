# Report a Job

Help keep the Seaneb community safe by reporting suspicious job listings or fraudulent employers.


![Report this Job Button on Job Details Page](/img/web/job-details.png)


### Zero-Tolerance Policy Against Job Scams
> **Remember:** Legitimate employers on Seaneb Jobs will **NEVER** ask you to pay registration charges, uniform fees, laptop security deposits, or document processing fees.

#### Report If an Employer:
- Demands any upfront payment.
- Offers unrealistic salaries for basic work (e.g., ₹70,000 for simple typing).
- Promotes multi-level marketing (MLM), crypto, or gambling apps.
- Behaves inappropriately during telephone screenings.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open the suspicious job listing.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the description and click "Report Job".
3. Choose the reason (e.g., "Demanded Money", "Fake Company", "Misleading Information").
4. Provide a brief description of what occurred.
5. Click "Submit Report".

### Expected Results
The post is flagged immediately for priority review by our Trust & Safety team.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Paid money to a fraudster** | File an immediate complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and notify your bank to stop the transaction. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Never share your bank account passwords, debit card PINs, or UPI PINs with anyone.
