# Why Can't I Apply?

Troubleshoot issues that may cause the Apply button to appear disabled or inactive.

### Top 5 Reasons & Fixes
1. **Incomplete Profile:** Certain jobs require basic education, experience, or city details before submission. Complete your profile to at least 60%.
2. **Already Applied:** You have already submitted an application to this specific posting. Check your Applied Jobs tab.
3. **Job Has Closed:** The employer marked the vacancy as filled or the 30-day listing period expired.
4. **Session Timeout:** Your login session expired. Refresh your browser and log in again via OTP.
5. **Location Restriction:** Some employers specify that candidates must live within a specific district or city.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Read the tooltip message on or near the disabled button.
2. Visit "My Profile" and complete any missing required fields.
3. Refresh your browser page and click "Apply Now" again.

### Expected Results
Resolution of application blockers.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Button says "Application Limit Reached"** | The employer has temporarily paused new incoming applications while reviewing existing candidates. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Keep your profile completion at 100% so you never encounter profile-related application restrictions.
