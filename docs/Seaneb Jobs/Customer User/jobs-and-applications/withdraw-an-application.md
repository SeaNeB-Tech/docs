# Withdraw an Application

How to cancel an application if you accepted another job offer or applied by mistake.

### When to Withdraw
If you found employment elsewhere or realized a job location is too far to commute, withdrawing your application informs the recruiter and keeps their candidate pipeline organized.

> **Note:** Withdrawing an application is permanent. You cannot re-apply to the same specific job posting once withdrawn.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Go to your "Applications" dashboard.
2. Find the job card you wish to cancel.
3. Click the "Withdraw Application" option.
4. Select a brief reason (e.g., "Accepted another offer" or "Applied by mistake").
5. Click "Confirm Withdrawal".

### Expected Results
The application is removed from the employer's active list and archived under your history.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Accidentally withdrew** | Contact the employer directly if their phone number was visible, or search for other open positions at the company. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** If an interview was scheduled for today, call the employer as a professional courtesy in addition to withdrawing online.
