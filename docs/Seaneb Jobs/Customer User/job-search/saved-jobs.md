# Saved Jobs

Bookmark vacancies to compare options and submit applications when you are ready.


![My Saved Jobs Dashboard](/img/web/savedjobs.png)


### Why Use Saved Jobs?
When browsing, you might find interesting positions that you want to review later or discuss with family before applying. Bookmarking them saves them to your account across all your devices.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Mobile App Experience

![Mobile View - Saved Jobs Collection Screen](/img/mobile/saved-jobs.png)

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Locate the bookmark/heart icon on any job card or job details page.
2. Click the bookmark icon to save the listing.
3. To view all saved jobs, click your profile menu and select "Saved Jobs".
4. Review your saved vacancies and click "Apply Now" when ready.
5. Click the bookmark icon again to remove a job from your saved list.

### Expected Results
Organized collection of prospective job listings stored in your profile.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A saved job disappeared** | If an employer closes a vacancy or it exceeds 30 days, it is automatically moved to expired listings. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Don't wait too long to apply to saved jobs, as local employers frequently hire candidates within the first few days of posting.
