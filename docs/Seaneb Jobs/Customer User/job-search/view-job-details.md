# View Job Details

Learn how to read and analyze job details pages to ensure you meet requirements before applying.


![Complete Job Details & Eligibility Criteria](/img/web/job-details.png)


### What to Inspect on a Job Listing
- **Role Summary:** Overview of daily tasks, working hours, and shift timings.
- **Compensation & Benefits:** Fixed salary, performance incentives, overtime pay, PF/ESI benefits.
- **Office Location:** Physical workplace address and nearest landmark.
- **Eligibility:** Minimum education, mandatory skills, language requirements, and vehicle ownership.
- **Employer Badge:** Look for the verified shield icon confirming the company has been vetted by Seaneb.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Click on any job card title from your search feed.
2. Read through the role description and responsibilities.
3. Check the "Requirements" section to ensure you meet the criteria.
4. Review the employer profile and operating hours.
5. Decide whether to "Apply Now", "Save Job", or "Share Job".

### Expected Results
You have full context about the position and can submit an informed application.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Job description seems vague** | Contact the employer via the contact options or submit screening questions upon applying. |
| **Job looks suspicious** | Click "Report a Job" at the bottom of the page. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Always check the working hours and commute distance to ensure the daily routine fits your schedule.
