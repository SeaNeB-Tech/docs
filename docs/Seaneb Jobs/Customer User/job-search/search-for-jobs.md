# Search for Jobs

Learn how to use the Seaneb Jobs search engine to discover verified vacancies in your neighborhood and city.


![Seaneb Jobs Search Bar on Homepage](/img/web/home.png)


![Available Jobs List in City](/img/web/job-list.png)


### Search Techniques
The Seaneb Jobs search bar allows you to look up opportunities using multiple criteria:
- **By Job Title:** Type designations like "Accountant", "Store Executive", "Receptionist", "Delivery Associate", or "Field Sales".
- **By Skill:** Search for tools like "Tally Prime", "AutoCAD", "Excel", or "Two Wheeler".
- **By Location:** Enter your city, district, or neighborhood area to prioritize nearby employers.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Visit the homepage or the Jobs directory page.
2. In the search box, enter your desired role or skill.
3. In the location box, select your city or local area.
4. Click "Search Jobs" to view matching vacancies.
5. Browse through the resulting job cards.

### Expected Results
A list of active job postings matching your query, complete with salary range, company name, and location details.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No jobs found** | Try broader keywords (for instance, search for "Sales" instead of "B2B Hardware Retail Salesperson"). |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Check the search feed daily as local employers post fresh vacancies each morning.
