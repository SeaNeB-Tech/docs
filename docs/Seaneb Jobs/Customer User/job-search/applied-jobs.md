# Applied Jobs

Track the complete history of every job application you have submitted on Seaneb Jobs.


![My Job Applications and Statuses](/img/web/applications.png)


### Features of the Applied Jobs Tab
- View the exact date and timestamp of your application.
- Monitor recruiter actions (whether your resume was viewed or shortlisted).
- Access employer contact information once an interview is scheduled.
- Keep track of past applications for your personal job search records.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Click on "Applications" in the main navigation bar.
2. Select the "Applied" tab.
3. Browse through your submitted applications.
4. Click on any application card to see the detailed hiring timeline.

### Expected Results
Clear overview of all employers who have received your resume.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cannot find a submitted application** | Ensure you were logged into the same mobile account when submitting the application. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Review your Applied Jobs list every 2-3 days to respond quickly to scheduled interview requests.
