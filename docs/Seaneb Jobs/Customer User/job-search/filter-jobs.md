# Filter Jobs

Refine your search results using smart filters for salary, experience, job type, and education.


![Job Search Filters (Salary, Work Mode, Experience)](/img/web/job-list.png)


### Available Filter Options
- **Job Type:** Full-time, Part-time, Work from Home, Internship, Contract.
- **Monthly Salary:** Filter by minimum take-home salary (₹10,000+, ₹15,000+, ₹25,000+, ₹40,000+).
- **Experience Level:** Fresher (0 years), 1-2 years, 3-5 years, 5+ years.
- **Education Requirement:** 10th Pass, 12th Pass, Graduate, Post Graduate, Diploma / ITI.
- **Date Posted:** Last 24 hours, Last 7 days, Last 30 days.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Mobile App Experience

![Mobile View - Local Job Search & Filter Results](/img/mobile/job-list.png)

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Perform an initial job search.
2. On mobile devices, tap the "Filters" button at the top of the job feed. On desktop, locate the filter panel on the left sidebar.
3. Select the checkboxes matching your criteria (e.g., "Full-time" and "Fresher").
4. Click "Apply Filters" to refresh the job list.
5. Click "Clear All" whenever you want to reset filters.

### Expected Results
The job list dynamically narrows down to only display positions meeting all selected criteria.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Zero results after filtering** | Uncheck one or two constraints (such as salary range) to widen the pool of matching jobs. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** If you have no prior experience, always check the "Fresher" filter to view employers willing to train on the job.
