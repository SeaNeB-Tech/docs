# Notification Not Received

Diagnose missing WhatsApp messages, SMS alerts, or silent app notifications.

### Checklist for Missing Notifications
1. **Check Notification Settings:** Go to Settings > Notifications and confirm switches are ON.
2. **Check Spam Folders:** Check your email spam folder for messages from @seaneb.com.
3. **Verify Phone Number:** Ensure your registered number matches your active phone SIM card.
4. **App Permissions:** If using the Seaneb web app on mobile, allow notifications in your device settings.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open "Settings" > "Notifications".
2. Turn ON all notification switches.
3. Save your preferences.
4. Check the in-app notification bell icon on the website for recent updates.

### Expected Results
Restoration of timely job and interview alerts.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **WhatsApp messages stopped** | Ensure you have not muted or blocked the Seaneb WhatsApp business account. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** You can always view all alerts in your in-app notification center (the bell icon) even if SMS is delayed.
