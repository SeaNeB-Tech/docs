# OTP Not Received

Fast troubleshooting steps if your mobile verification code is delayed or not arriving.


![OTP Verification Screen and Resend Timer](/img/web/otp.png)


### Quick Diagnostic Steps
1. **Verify Phone Number:** Ensure you entered the correct 10 digits without country codes (+91) or leading zeros.
2. **Check Cellular Signal:** Move to an area with stable mobile network coverage.
3. **Wait for Countdown:** Allow the 60-second timer to finish before requesting a new code.
4. **Check SMS Inbox Storage:** Ensure your SMS inbox is not full.
5. **Inspect Spam / Promotions Folder:** Some smartphone messaging apps automatically filter automated SMS into spam folders.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Wait for the 60-second countdown to expire.
2. Click the "Resend OTP" button.
3. If WhatsApp OTP is displayed as an alternative, choose "Send via WhatsApp".
4. Restart your mobile phone to reset your telecom connection.

### Expected Results
Prompt receipt of your 6-digit verification code.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Persistent non-delivery** | Contact your telecom operator (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL) to verify that service/transactional SMS is not blocked on your number. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** WhatsApp OTP is usually delivered in under 5 seconds if SMS is delayed.
