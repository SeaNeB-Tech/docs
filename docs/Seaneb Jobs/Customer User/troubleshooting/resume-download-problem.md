# Resume Download Problem

Fix PDF generation issues, browser download blockers, and corrupted files.

### Troubleshooting Steps
1. **Allow Browser Popups:** Look at your address bar for a blocked popup icon and select "Always allow downloads from jobs.seaneb.com".
2. **Check Device Storage:** Ensure your phone or computer has sufficient free storage space.
3. **Alternative Print to PDF:** Click "Preview Resume", press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac), and choose "Save as PDF".

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Enable downloads and popups for jobs.seaneb.com in browser settings.
2. Click "Preview Resume" to verify the document renders on screen.
3. Click "Download PDF" again.

### Expected Results
A clean, high-resolution PDF resume saved to your device.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Blank white PDF downloaded** | Ensure all profile sections have saved text without special unsupported symbols. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox offer the most reliable PDF download performance on mobile devices.
