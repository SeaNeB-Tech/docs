# Payment Failed

Guidance regarding payment queries and transaction protections on Seaneb.

### Job Seekers Never Pay!
> **Important Notice:** Job seekers never pay for any feature on Seaneb Jobs. Searching, applying, profile building, and resume downloads are 100% free.

This guide applies only to **business employers** purchasing job credits or featured vacancy promotions.

#### If an Employer Payment Fails:
- **Bank Reversals:** Failed UPI and card payments are automatically reversed by your issuing bank within 3 to 5 business days.
- **Reconciliation Delay:** Banking gateways may take up to 15 minutes to confirm status during peak banking hours.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Check your bank statement for a 12-digit UPI UTR number.
2. Wait 15 minutes to see if credits reflect in your employer dashboard.
3. If credits do not appear, email billing@seaneb.com with your UTR reference number.

### Expected Results
Prompt credit allocation or automated banking refund.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A recruiter asked a candidate for money** | Report them immediately via "Report a Fake Job". This is a violation of our platform rules. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Do not initiate repeated transactions if your payment screen hangs; wait for the first transaction to settle.
