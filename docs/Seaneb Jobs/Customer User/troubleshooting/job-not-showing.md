# Job Not Showing

Understand why a specific job post or searched vacancy might not appear in your feed.


![Job Feed and Filtering by Area](/img/web/job-list.png)


### Reasons a Job May Not Appear
1. **Job Has Closed or Expired:** Vacancies close once filled or after 30 days.
2. **Filters Too Strict:** An active filter (like salary minimum or commute radius) may be hiding the listing.
3. **Pending Verification:** Newly created posts undergo automated review before appearing publicly.
4. **Different City Selected:** Verify your location filter matches the job location.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Click "Clear All Filters" on the search results screen.
2. Search directly by the employer or company name.
3. Check your location filter to ensure you are searching in the right city.

### Expected Results
Discovery of the target vacancy if it is currently active.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Job was visible yesterday but gone today** | The employer likely marked the vacancy as filled and closed the listing. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Save jobs you are interested in so you can track them even if search filters change.
