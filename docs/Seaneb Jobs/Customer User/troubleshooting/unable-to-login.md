# Unable to Login

Fix login errors, session timeouts, and browser cookie restrictions.


![Seaneb Jobs Login Portal](/img/web/login.png)


### Common Login Solutions
If your OTP is accepted but you cannot reach your dashboard:
- **Clear Browser Cache:** Stale cookies can interfere with login sessions.
- **Check Cookie Permissions:** Ensure your browser is set to allow first-party cookies.
- **Disable Aggressive VPNs / Adblockers:** Certain privacy extensions block authentication API endpoints.
- **Try Another Browser:** Test in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open your browser settings.
2. Clear cached data and cookies for jobs.seaneb.com.
3. Close and reopen your browser.
4. Visit https://jobs.seaneb.com/ and log in again.

### Expected Results
Successful authentication and navigation to your user dashboard.
