# Unable to Apply

Resolve errors preventing application submissions, missing required fields, and profile criteria.

### Solutions for Common Apply Errors
- **Incomplete Profile:** Complete your profile to at least 60% before applying.
- **Already Applied:** Check your Applied Jobs tab to confirm if you already submitted your resume.
- **Expired Session:** Refresh your browser and re-authenticate via OTP.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Read the error message displayed near the Apply button.
2. Open "My Profile" and complete any missing required fields.
3. Refresh the job post page and click "Apply Now".

### Expected Results
Successful submission of your application to the employer.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Button says "Application Limit Reached"** | The employer has capped applications for this opening. Check back later or apply to similar vacancies. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Keep your profile 100% complete so you can apply to any job with one click.
