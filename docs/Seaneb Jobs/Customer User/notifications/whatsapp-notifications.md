# WhatsApp Notifications

Receive convenient, real-time interview invitations and venue map links directly on WhatsApp.

### Benefits of WhatsApp Alerts
- Clickable Google Maps links to interview venues.
- Instant alerts when an employer shortlists your profile.
- Easy two-way attendance confirmation directly in your chat.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. In "Settings" > "Notifications", toggle "WhatsApp Updates" to ON.
2. Verify that your registered Seaneb mobile number is active on WhatsApp.
3. You will receive a welcome confirmation message from our verified Seaneb WhatsApp account.

### Expected Results
Seamless, rich notification delivery directly into your WhatsApp chats.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Not receiving WhatsApp messages** | Ensure you did not block the official Seaneb WhatsApp business number. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** You can reply "STOP" at any time to opt out of promotional alerts while keeping essential interview messages.
