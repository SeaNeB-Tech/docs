# How to Manage Notifications

Customise channels, message frequency, and privacy preferences for all platform alerts.

![Mobile View - Notification Alerts Center](/img/mobile/notifications.png)


### Full Notification Controls
You can independently turn ON or OFF:
- WhatsApp notifications
- Email job digests
- Marketing announcements
- SMS interview alerts (recommended to keep ON for timely interview updates)

> **Note:** Critical transactional security messages (like login OTPs) cannot be disabled for account protection.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Go to user menu > "Settings".
2. Select "Notifications" from the settings tabs.
3. Adjust the switches for WhatsApp, Email, and SMS.
4. Click "Save Notification Settings" at the bottom.

### Expected Results
Your customized notification schedule is updated instantly across our servers.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Changes did not take effect** | Ensure you clicked "Save Notification Settings" before leaving the page. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Keep WhatsApp and SMS alerts enabled while job searching so you never miss an interview opportunity.
