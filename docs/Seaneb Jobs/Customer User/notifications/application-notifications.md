# Application Notifications

Understand the alerts triggered as your submitted applications progress through hiring stages.

### Notification Milestones
- **Application Submitted:** Confirms employer received your resume.
- **Application Viewed:** Alerts you that the recruiter opened your profile.
- **Shortlisted:** Informs you that you made the shortlist.
- **Interview Scheduled:** Delivers date, time, and location for your interview.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Click the bell icon on the top right navigation to open your In-App Notification Center.
2. Tap any notification to open the relevant application card.
3. Review recruiter instructions or interview details.

### Expected Results
Immediate awareness of employer interest and interview commitments.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Missed in-app alert** | All past notifications remain saved under the bell icon for 30 days. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Check the bell icon every morning during your job search.
