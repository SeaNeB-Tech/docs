# Job Alerts

Set up custom job alerts to be notified immediately when matching vacancies are published in your area.

![Mobile View - Job Alert Notifications Feed](/img/mobile/notifications.png)


### Why Set Up Alerts?
Being among the first 10 candidates to apply for a freshly posted job significantly increases your interview callback rate. Custom alerts automate this discovery.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Perform a job search with your desired role and city.
2. Click the "Create Job Alert for this Search" button above the results.
3. Choose alert delivery channel: WhatsApp, SMS, or Email.
4. Select alert frequency: Daily or Immediate.
5. Click "Save Alert".

### Expected Results
Automatic notifications whenever an employer posts a matching job opening.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Too many alerts** | Go to Settings > Job Alerts to adjust frequency or delete alerts you no longer need. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** You can create up to 5 concurrent job alerts for different roles (e.g., one for "Receptionist" and another for "Office Executive").
