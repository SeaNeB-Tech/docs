# SMS Notifications

Understand instant SMS alerts for login OTPs, urgent interview calls, and application updates.

### Messages Sent via SMS
- Instant 6-digit OTP codes for secure login.
- Real-time confirmation when an employer schedules an interview.
- Critical account security notices.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Verify that your 10-digit registered mobile number is active.
2. Check that your cellular provider does not block transactional service SMS.

### Expected Results
Instant receipt of time-sensitive alerts directly on your phone.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SMS delays** | Verify cellular signal strength and restart your phone to clear temporary SMS queue delays. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** SMS is our fastest channel for interview notices; keep your SIM card active and charged.
