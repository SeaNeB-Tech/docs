# Email Notifications

Manage transactional emails, application receipts, and weekly job alerts delivered to your inbox.

### Types of Email Notifications
- **Application Receipts:** Confirmation when your application is delivered.
- **Shortlist & Interview Notices:** Official interview invitations with date, venue, and HR contact person.
- **Weekly Job Match Digest:** Personalized summary of newly posted vacancies matching your preferences.
- **Security Alerts:** Logins from new devices or email address modifications.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Ensure your email address is added and verified in your profile.
2. Go to "Settings" > "Notifications".
3. Toggle "Email Notifications" ON or OFF.
4. Click "Save Preferences".

### Expected Results
Receipt of detailed notifications in your email inbox.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Emails going to spam** | Check your Spam or Junk folder and add support@seaneb.com to your contacts or safe sender list. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Check your email inbox daily during an active job hunt.
