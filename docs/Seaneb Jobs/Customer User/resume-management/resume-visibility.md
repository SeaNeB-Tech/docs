# Resume Visibility

Control whether your resume is discoverable by employers in candidate talent searches.

### Visibility Options
1. **Public to Verified Employers (Recommended):** Vetted employers searching for candidates in your city can view your qualifications and invite you to apply. Your mobile number is hidden until you accept an interview invite.
2. **Private (Applied Jobs Only):** Only employers of jobs you explicitly apply to can view your resume. Your profile is invisible in general talent searches.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Go to "Settings" > "Privacy & Visibility".
2. Choose between "Public to Verified Employers" and "Private".
3. Click "Save Preferences".

### Expected Results
Your visibility settings take effect immediately across all employer candidate searches.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Getting too many calls from recruiters** | Switch your visibility to "Private" or turn off the "Seeking Job" toggle. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Keep visibility Public when actively looking for work to receive proactive interview offers.
