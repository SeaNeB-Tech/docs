# Create Your Resume

Use the built-in 100% Free Resume Builder on Seaneb Jobs to create an ATS-friendly, professional PDF resume.

![Mobile View - Educational Qualifications Entry Screen](/img/mobile/education.png)


### Why Use the Seaneb Resume Builder?
Unlike third-party websites that charge subscription fees after you spend time designing a resume, Seaneb provides a completely free resume generator.

- **ATS-Friendly Formatting:** Designed to be easily read by automated scanners and human recruiters.
- **Auto-Sync:** Updates made to your profile automatically refresh your resume.
- **Clean Structure:** Standard A4 layout, clear headings, balanced spacing, and professional fonts.
- **Zero Watermarks:** Download crisp PDFs without platform watermarks.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Log in and navigate to "My Profile".
2. Ensure your work experience, education, and skills are filled in.
3. Click "Resume Builder" or "Download Resume".
4. Choose your preferred resume template layout.
5. Click "Preview Resume" to inspect formatting.
6. Click "Download PDF" to save it to your device.

### Expected Results
A polished, job-ready resume saved to your phone or computer.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sections appear blank in resume** | Return to "My Profile" and verify that your education and experience entries are saved. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Keep job descriptions concise with 2-3 bullet points highlighting what you achieved in each role.
