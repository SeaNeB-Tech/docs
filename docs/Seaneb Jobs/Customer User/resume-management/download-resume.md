# Download Resume

How to download and print your professional PDF resume for job interviews and offline applications.

### Uses for Your Downloaded PDF Resume
- Bring printed copies to in-person interviews.
- Share via WhatsApp with business owners and hiring managers.
- Attach to emails or job applications across any external platform.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open "My Profile".
2. Click the blue "Download Resume" button in the header.
3. The browser downloads your formatted PDF document immediately.
4. Locate the file in your device's "Downloads" folder.

### Expected Results
A crisp, high-resolution PDF document ready for sharing or printing.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Download does not start** | Check if your mobile browser blocked automatic popups or downloads. Tap "Allow" when prompted. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Send the downloaded PDF to your email so you always have a copy accessible from any phone or cyber café.
