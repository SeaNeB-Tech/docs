# Update Resume

Keep your resume updated as you gain new skills, complete courses, or change jobs.

![Mobile View - Work Experience Details Screen](/img/mobile/experince.png)


### How Resume Updates Work
- **If using the Seaneb Resume Builder:** Updating your work experience or skills on "My Profile" automatically refreshes your resume.
- **If using an Uploaded PDF:** Simply upload a new version to replace the existing document.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Navigate to "My Profile".
2. Edit your work history, certifications, or telephone number.
3. Click "Save Profile".
4. Click "Download Resume" to generate a refreshed PDF.

### Expected Results
Your resume reflects your latest career progression.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Old details still showing in download** | Clear your browser cache or re-download using private browsing mode. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Review and update your resume every 3 to 6 months even if you are not actively looking for work.
