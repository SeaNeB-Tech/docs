# Resume Tips

Expert guidance on optimizing your resume to impress local employers and secure interviews.

### Top 5 Resume Writing Recommendations
1. **Quantify Achievements:** Instead of writing "Handled shop sales", write "Achieved ₹8 Lakh monthly retail sales and managed inventory for 500+ SKUs".
2. **List Software & Technical Tools:** Mention programs you know (e.g., Tally Prime, Excel, Photoshop, AutoCAD, Busy).
3. **Mention Local Languages:** If you speak regional languages (Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu), include them as local customer-facing roles value bilingual staff.
4. **Use Reverse Chronological Order:** Always list your current or most recent job first.
5. **Verify Contact Information:** Ensure your telephone number and email are 100% typo-free.

### Prerequisites
- An active Seaneb Jobs account.
- A stable internet connection on your phone or computer.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Review each past job entry on your profile.
2. Add 2-3 specific accomplishments for each position.
3. Check spelling of company names and educational degrees.
4. Preview your resume before downloading.

### Expected Results
A competitive, professional resume that sets you apart from other applicants.

### Common Problems & Solutions
| Problem | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Resume is too long** | Keep your resume focused on the last 5-7 years of work experience; 1-2 pages is ideal. |

### Helpful Tips
> **Tip:** Proofread your resume carefully before sending it to employers.
