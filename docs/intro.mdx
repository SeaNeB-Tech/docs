# Welcome to Seaneb

Welcome to **Seaneb Documentation** — your central resource for understanding, developing, integrating, and managing the Seaneb ecosystem.

**Seaneb Technologies Pvt. Ltd.** builds digital solutions that connect people, businesses, and services through technology.

## About Seaneb

Seaneb is a technology ecosystem designed to simplify everyday activities and empower businesses through digital platforms.

Our products include:

- **Seaneb Jobs** — Discover job opportunities and connect with employers.
- **Seaneb Tiffin** — Find local tiffin services and food providers.
- **Seaneb Auto** — Explore second-hand vehicles.
- **Seaneb Property** — Discover real estate and property listings.
- **Seaneb News** — Access news and updates.
- **Seaneb NGO** — Connect with nonprofit organizations.
- **Seaneb Offers** — Discover local business offers and deals.

3. Install project dependencies.
